⚠️ Red Flags of Loan Scams & Phishing Websites

Fraudulent websites often lure victims with promises of instant loans, then ask for a 4-digit banking PIN, ATM PIN, or online banking password under the guise of "verifying internet banking." They then steal these credentials to drain victim accounts.

Legitimate vs. Fraudulent Lenders

Aspect Legitimate Lender (SmartLoan) Phishing Website
Banking Credentials Account # only for deposit Asks for Banking PIN / Password
Disclosures Clear APR and repayment schedule Hidden fees, no terms breakdown
Data Transmission Encrypted SOC-2 API backend Exfiltrates to Telegram / Webhooks
Credit Verification Regulated Credit Bureau / KYC Fake approval without underwriting
